Travelogues

My vision with Pixelated Pictures was not to confine it in boundaries of certain type of story driven film making. From 2017, the new direction was to shoot snippets on travels and create a certain element of narrative to comprehensively demonstrate experience around places. The structure was lucid, ranging from B-roll footage to Vlog format, with distinct treatment in every project.

Goa | Candolim – Aguada – Baga – Ozran – Arpora – Morjim – Cavelossim – Betalbatim

Kerala | Cochin – Munnar

Cape Town | Aquila Reserve – Table Mountain – Signal Hill – Bo Kaap – V&A Waterfront

Himachal | Shimla – Kulu – Manali – Mandi – Dharamsala – Triund

Thailand | Bangkok – Ko Samui – Koh Tao

Maharashtra | Srivardhan – Diveagar – Murud
