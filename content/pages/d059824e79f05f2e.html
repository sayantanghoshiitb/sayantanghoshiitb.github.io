Shorts

Back in 2013, Pixelated Pictures was launched as an experimental project by few youngsters to tell the stories they wanted to share. However experience was minimal with the technicalities to the techniques of the craft. Additionally operating at shoestring budget was not a boon either. That did not deter them and as time progressed, the project ended up becoming banner for different dreams for different creators who do not necessarily share the same language. Operating mostly under drama and thriller genre, the shorts have spanned across languages and treatment.
